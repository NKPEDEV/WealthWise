Airdrop Safety Tips

1. Verify Official Sources

Use verified project websites and official announcements.

2. Never Share Your Recovery Phrase

No legitimate airdrop should require your wallet seed phrase or private key.

3. Watch Out for Fake Websites

Check URLs carefully and avoid suspicious links and unexpected wallet connection requests.

4. Understand the Risks

Airdrops are not guaranteed income. Some tokens may have no market value.
